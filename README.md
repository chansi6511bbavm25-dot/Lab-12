### LAB 12 – AI Presentation Lab: Deck Generation, Professionalism Audit and Risk Triage

This lab focused on creating and evaluating an AI-assisted presentation on **“Improving Passenger Experience at Airports Using Generative AI.”** The objective was to understand how Generative AI can support airport passenger-service activities while maintaining accuracy, privacy, professionalism, and human responsibility.

The first step was to prepare a **presentation brief** covering the business problem, target audience, objective, slide count, key topics, visual style, tone, and information requiring verification. The target audience was the airport management team, and the presentation was planned for 10 slides. The content focused on passenger-service challenges, GenAI use cases, workflows, flight-delay communication, benefits, risks, governance, implementation, and conclusion.

An AI-generated outline was then developed using a **mechanism-first structure**. The presentation explained how verified information is provided to AI, how AI creates a draft, and how employees review and approve the output before communication. A flight-delay communication example was included to demonstrate a practical aviation use case.

The presentation was reviewed through a **content accuracy audit, professionalism audit, and visual audit**. Unsupported statistics, fake charts, guaranteed benefits, and unverified operational information were identified as potential risks. These were removed or marked for verification. Special attention was given to hallucination, privacy risks, outdated information, and overreliance on AI.

A **severity × risk triage** was also performed to prioritise high-risk issues. Slides were revised to make human review, governance, and potential benefits clearer. The final presentation maintained a professional blue, white, and neutral-grey aviation/business style.

Overall, the lab demonstrated that AI can help with presentation planning, content organisation, and visual ideas, but **human judgement remains essential**. AI-generated content should be checked for accuracy, privacy, relevance, and business suitability before being used in an aviation environment. The final activity was prepared for upload to the `genai-business-portfolio/day-12-ai-presentation` GitHub folder.
